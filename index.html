The Tools

Free & open source · one at a time

HopperDock Live

A floating toolbar that tiles your windows, remembers exactly where they go, and launches the scripts you actually use.

Download Source Guide

TriggerWord Days away

Say a word on stream, fire a sound. Whisper listens to what you actually say — no hotkey, no chat command.

Source drops with the release.

Bunny Deck Coming soon

A Stream Deck without the Stream Deck. Turns a spare tablet or phone into a grid of shortcut buttons — no expensive hardware.

Being packaged for release.

Clip Hopper Coming soon

Finds the highlights in your match — the kills — and cuts each one out for you, on your own GPU. Built for Population: ONE.

In the works.

Stream Hopper In testing

One panel to launch and manage the win songs, the win detector and the live clip grabber.

Needs more testing.

GPU Monitor Coming soon

An always-on-top widget showing every GPU’s load, VRAM and temperature — and which app is actually eating it.

In the works.

Voicemeeter Mini Coming soon

Voicemeeter is great but way too big. This is better — just the faders and mutes, in a strip you can leave on screen.

In the works.

Video Downloader Coming soon

Paste a link, get the video. Plain VOD and clip pulling for people who don’t want a browser extension.

In the works.
